# Scientific Discovery Assurance Framework (SDAF) v2

EvalForge extends beyond ordinary LLM scoring with a research-assurance layer for AI-generated scientific claims. The goal is not to let software declare scientific truth. The goal is to make candidate claims, evidence, verification, provenance, uncertainty, review, and post-publication status explicit and auditable.

## Status semantics

Every criterion uses one of six states:

- `PASS`
- `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS`
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `FAIL`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`
- `UNKNOWN`

`UNKNOWN != FAIL`, but EvalForge fails closed on unknown criteria by default. `NOT_APPLICABLE != PASS` and is excluded from aggregation. A gate containing only not-applicable criteria is itself `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Gate sequence

```text
Problem Definition
        ↓
Candidate Generation
        ↓
Falsification
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Statistical Validation
        ↓
Generalization
        ↓
Novelty / Prior Art
        ↓
Reproducibility
        ↓
Independent Replication
        ↓
Provenance / Contamination
        ↓
Research Integrity
        ↓
Safety / Ethics
        ↓
Expert Review
        ↓
DISCOVERY_CLAIM_READY
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Continuous External Replication
        ↓
Correction / Retraction
        ↓
DISCOVERY_STILL_VALID
```

## Canonical gates

### Problem readiness

```text
PROBLEM_READY =
PROBLEM_FORMALIZED
AND SCOPE_DEFINED
AND CLAIM_TYPE_DEFINED
AND SUCCESS_CRITERIA_DEFINED
AND FAILURE_CRITERIA_DEFINED
AND ASSUMPTIONS_EXPLICIT
AND BOUNDARY_CONDITIONS_DEFINED
AND EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_PREDEFINED
AND ACCEPTANCE_THRESHOLDS_DEFINED
AND PROHIBITED_SHORTCUTS_DEFINED
```

### Candidate validity and falsification

```text
CANDIDATE_VALID =
PROBLEM_READY
AND NOVEL_CANDIDATE
AND CLAIMS_MACHINE_READABLE
AND INTERNAL_CONSISTENCY_PASS
AND EVIDENCE_TRACEABLE
AND DERIVATION_TRACEABLE
AND NO_UNRESOLVED_CRITICAL_ERROR
AND CLAIM_SCOPE_WITHIN_ASSUMPTIONS

FALSIFICATION_PASS =
CANDIDATE_VALID
AND ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW_PASS
AND COUNTEREXAMPLE_SEARCH_PASS
AND EDGE_CASE_SEARCH_PASS
AND CONTRADICTION_SEARCH_PASS
AND ASSUMPTION_VIOLATION_TEST_PASS
AND PROPERTY_BASED_TEST_PASS
AND METAMORPHIC_TEST_PASS
AND NO_KNOWN_FATAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE
```

### Verification and statistics

```text
VERIFICATION_SUCCESS =
FALSIFICATION_PASS
AND INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_PASS
AND FORMAL_CHECK_PASS
AND SYMBOLIC_CHECK_PASS
AND NUMERICAL_CHECK_PASS
AND DIMENSIONAL_CONSISTENCY_PASS
AND BASELINE_COMPARISON_PASS
AND ROBUSTNESS_CHECK_PASS
AND SENSITIVITY_ANALYSIS_PASS
AND UNCERTAINTY_QUANTIFIED
AND STATISTICAL_VALIDITY_PASS
AND NO_KNOWN_CONTRADICTION

STATISTICAL_VALIDITY_PASS =
SAMPLE_SIZE_JUSTIFIED
AND EFFECT_SIZE_REPORTED
AND CONFIDENCE_INTERVALS_REPORTED
AND MULTIPLE_HYPOTHESIS_CONTROL_PASS
AND TEST_ASSUMPTIONS_CHECKED
AND MODEL_SELECTION_BIAS_CONTROLLED
AND DATA_SNOOPING_CONTROLLED
AND LEAKAGE_CHECK_PASS
AND UNCERTAINTY_CALIBRATED
AND NEGATIVE_RESULTS_RECORDED
```

Domain-specific checks should be marked `NOT_APPLICABLE`, not faked as `PASS`.

### Generalization and novelty

```text
GENERALIZATION_SUCCESS =
VERIFICATION_SUCCESS
AND OUT_OF_SAMPLE_VALIDATION_PASS
AND HOLDOUT_INTEGRITY_PASS
AND TEMPORAL_VALIDATION_PASS
AND EXTERNAL_DATASET_VALIDATION_PASS
AND DISTRIBUTION_SHIFT_TEST_PASS
AND STRESS_TEST_PASS
AND BOUNDARY_CONDITIONS_TESTED
AND REGIME_ANALYSIS_PASS
AND FAILURE_ENVELOPE_DEFINED
AND EXTERNAL_VALIDITY_DOCUMENTED

NOVELTY_VALIDATED =
GENERALIZATION_SUCCESS
AND SYSTEMATIC_LITERATURE_SEARCH_PASS
AND PRIOR_ART_CHECK_PASS
AND DUPLICATION_CHECK_PASS
AND CLOSEST_PRIOR_WORK_IDENTIFIED
AND DELTA_OVER_PRIOR_WORK_EXPLICIT
AND NOVELTY_CLAIM_CALIBRATED
AND SOURCE_ATTRIBUTION_COMPLETE
```

Novelty and correctness are separate. Failure to find prior art is not proof of novelty.

### Reproducibility and independent replication

```text
REPRODUCIBILITY_PASS =
NOVELTY_VALIDATED
AND CODE_AVAILABLE
AND DATA_AVAILABLE_OR_JUSTIFIED
AND CONFIGURATION_CAPTURED
AND DEPENDENCIES_PINNED
AND ENVIRONMENT_REPRODUCIBLE
AND RANDOM_SEEDS_RECORDED
AND HARDWARE_REQUIREMENTS_RECORDED
AND EXECUTION_INSTRUCTIONS_COMPLETE
AND EXPECTED_OUTPUTS_DEFINED
AND ARTIFACT_HASHES_VERIFIED
AND CLEAN_ROOM_REPRODUCTION_PASS

REPLICATION_PASS =
REPRODUCIBILITY_PASS
AND INDEPENDENT_TEAM_USED
AND INDEPENDENT_ENVIRONMENT_USED
AND INDEPENDENT_IMPLEMENTATION_USED
AND RESULTS_WITHIN_ACCEPTANCE_RANGE
AND DISCREPANCIES_EXPLAINED
AND INDEPENDENT_REPRODUCTION_PASS
```

Reproduction asks whether the published method and artifacts reproduce the result. Replication asks whether independent work supports the scientific conclusion.

### Provenance and contamination

```text
PROVENANCE_COMPLETE =
DATA_LINEAGE_COMPLETE
AND SOURCE_ATTRIBUTION_COMPLETE
AND LICENSES_RECORDED
AND DATA_ACQUISITION_TIME_RECORDED
AND MODEL_PROVIDER_RECORDED
AND MODEL_VERSION_RECORDED
AND MODEL_CONFIGURATION_RECORDED
AND SYSTEM_PROMPTS_RECORDED
AND RESEARCH_PROTOCOL_RECORDED
AND TOOL_CALLS_RECORDED
AND RETRIEVED_CONTEXT_RECORDED
AND EXTERNAL_API_RESPONSES_TRACED
AND COMPUTE_ENVIRONMENT_RECORDED
AND RANDOM_SEEDS_RECORDED
AND HUMAN_INTERVENTIONS_RECORDED
AND HUMAN_DECISIONS_ATTRIBUTED
AND ARTIFACT_HASHES_VERIFIED
AND TIMESTAMPS_RECORDED
AND CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_COMPLETE
AND DECISION_LOG_COMPLETE

PROVENANCE_INTEGRITY_PASS =
PROVENANCE_COMPLETE
AND TRAIN_TEST_CONTAMINATION_CHECK_PASS
AND BENCHMARK_CONTAMINATION_CHECK_PASS
AND PRIVATE_DATA_USAGE_DECLARED
AND UNPUBLISHED_SOURCE_USAGE_DECLARED
AND RETRIEVAL_CONTAMINATION_CHECK_PASS
AND DATA_LICENSE_COMPLIANCE_PASS
AND ATTRIBUTION_CONFLICT_CHECK_PASS
```

This is critical for AI-assisted discovery because a system must distinguish a novel derivation from memorized, retrieved, leaked, or unpublished prior work as far as the available evidence permits.

### Research integrity and responsible research

```text
RESEARCH_INTEGRITY_PASS =
PROVENANCE_INTEGRITY_PASS
AND NO_DATA_FABRICATION
AND NO_RESULT_FABRICATION
AND NO_CHERRY_PICKING
AND NO_HIDDEN_EXCLUSIONS
AND NO_UNDISCLOSED_POST_HOC_HYPOTHESES
AND NEGATIVE_RESULTS_PRESERVED
AND CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST_RECORDED
AND FUNDING_DISCLOSED
AND AUTHORSHIP_ATTRIBUTION_COMPLETE
AND AI_CONTRIBUTION_DISCLOSED
AND LIMITATIONS_DOCUMENTED
AND THREATS_TO_VALIDITY_DOCUMENTED

RESPONSIBLE_RESEARCH_PASS =
RESEARCH_INTEGRITY_PASS
AND ETHICS_REQUIREMENT_EVALUATED
AND SAFETY_REQUIREMENT_EVALUATED
AND PRIVACY_REQUIREMENT_EVALUATED
AND DUAL_USE_RISK_ASSESSED
AND HUMAN_SUBJECTS_COMPLIANCE_PASS
AND DATA_PROTECTION_PASS
AND RISK_MITIGATIONS_DOCUMENTED
```

### Research and discovery claim gates

```text
RESEARCH_READY =
REPLICATION_PASS
AND RESPONSIBLE_RESEARCH_PASS
AND PROVENANCE_COMPLETE
AND METHOD_TRACE_COMPLETE
AND COMPUTE_TRACE_COMPLETE
AND FAILURE_CASES_DOCUMENTED
AND CLAIM_LIMITATIONS_DOCUMENTED
AND EXPERT_REVIEW_PASS
AND REVIEW_OBJECTIONS_RESOLVED
AND OPEN_CRITICAL_ISSUES_ZERO

DISCOVERY_CLAIM_READY =
RESEARCH_READY
AND CLAIMS_MATCH_EVIDENCE
AND CLAIM_SCOPE_NOT_OVERSTATED
AND NOVELTY_CLAIM_SUPPORTED
AND ATTRIBUTION_COMPLETE
AND INDEPENDENT_EXPERT_CONFIRMATION
AND REPRODUCTION_INSTRUCTIONS_COMPLETE
AND PUBLICATION_PACKAGE_COMPLETE
AND ARTIFACTS_ARCHIVED
AND VERSION_PINNED
AND IMMUTABLE_IDENTIFIER_READY
```

`DISCOVERY_CLAIM_READY` means the evidence package is ready for scientific scrutiny. It does not mean EvalForge has proven a discovery to be universally true.

### Post-publication assurance

```text
POST_PUBLICATION_VALID =
DISCOVERY_CLAIM_READY
AND EXTERNAL_REPLICATION_STATUS_TRACKED
AND EXTERNAL_CRITIQUES_TRACKED
AND NEW_COUNTEREVIDENCE_TRACKED
AND ERRATA_PROCESS_DEFINED
AND CORRECTION_PROCESS_DEFINED
AND RETRACTION_CRITERIA_DEFINED
AND CLAIM_VERSIONING_ENABLED
AND ARTIFACT_VERSIONING_ENABLED
AND CURRENT_EVIDENCE_STILL_SUPPORTS_CLAIM

DISCOVERY_STILL_VALID =
POST_PUBLICATION_VALID
AND NO_NEW_FATAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE
AND NO_REPLICATION_FAILURE_UNRESOLVED
AND NO_PROVENANCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE
AND NO_CRITICAL_FORMAL_VERIFICATION_FAILURE
AND NO_RESEARCH_INTEGRITY_VIOLATION
```

## Implementation

The initial implementation lives in `app/research_assurance.py` and intentionally focuses on gate semantics and canonical criteria. It does not pretend to automate scientific judgment. Later releases can persist criterion evidence, expose gate APIs, add provenance manifests, connect formal/numerical checkers, and enforce selected gates in CI.

Suggested next milestones:

1. Persist assurance assessments and evidence references in SQLite.
2. Add `POST /api/research/assessments` and `GET /api/research/assessments/{id}`.
3. Add signed JSON provenance manifests and artifact hashes.
4. Add CI policy gate output suitable for GitHub pull requests.
5. Add independent verifier adapters (formal, symbolic, numerical, statistical).
6. Add a research dashboard showing gate failures, limitations, unknowns, and reviewer decisions.
